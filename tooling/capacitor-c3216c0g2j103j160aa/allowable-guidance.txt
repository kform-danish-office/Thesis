The allowable voltage and current graphs displayed in the detailed information within the TDK Product
Center are created based on the following conditions.

   •   The applied waveform is a sine wave of a single frequency.
   •   The evaluation environment for the self-heating temperature is at room temperature under natural
       convection.
   •   The board on which the MLCC is mounted is assumed to have sufficient heat dissipation for the
       applied current and to reach thermal equilibrium under natural convection.
   •   Under the above conditions, the allowable voltage and current are defined as those at which the
       self-heating temperature of the MLCC remains below 20°C.
   •   This data is for reference only and does not guarantee reliability.
