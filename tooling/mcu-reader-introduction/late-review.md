# Reader-first pin and MCU wording review

Read-only scan of Chapters 4–6 and 9–12 and appendix prose. No thesis source was edited. The planned introductory primer resolves global first use, but the following locations still benefit from naming the physical function before the identifier. Proposed wording preserves the existing G071/F410 assignments and measured quantities.

1. **Chapters/04_Topology_Analysis/01_Topology_Overview.tex:14** — First analysis paragraph uses PA10, PA1, PA0 and PA2 as if their functions are self-evident. Suggested opening: “The controller changes the length of switching bursts through its enable output, PA10 on the G071. The conditioned secondary-bulk voltage enters at PA1. The conditioned primary-voltage signal at PA0 provides optional feed-forward, while the primary-current signal at PA2 is used to distinguish loaded from no-load operation.” Keep the existing carrier quantity and explanation of average transfer. Do not imply that an unconditioned high voltage reaches an MCU pin.

2. **Chapters/04_Topology_Analysis/09_Device_Stress.tex:14–18** — “PA2 indicates…” and “PA10 inhibition…” hide the signal type. Prefer “The current-sense voltage at the controller input PA2 represents primary half-bridge current after calibration…” and “Driving the G071 burst-enable output PA10 low ends further commanded excitation…”. Keep the existing distinction between stored energy and commanded transfer, and between primary and connector currents.

3. **Chapters/05_Modeling_Simulation/07_Startup_NoLoad.tex:21** — “When PA10 is inhibited” is opaque to a beginner. Prefer “When the burst-enable output PA10 is held low, no further switching bursts are commanded…”. The rest of the paragraph correctly explains stored energy and should remain.

4. **Chapters/09_Experimental_Methods/04_Instrumentation.tex:4–7** — Expand the meaning of the internal records rather than naming only ADC/UART. Suggested: “The controller’s converted sensor readings and serial status messages were used to check sensing and commands. These ADC readings and UART messages were not treated as independent measurements of connector output.” Before the channel-assignment table, explain that CH1–CH4 are oscilloscope input channels, unlike the MCU’s port labels. Do not change probe ratios, assignments or instrument data.

5. **Chapters/09_Experimental_Methods/05_Probing.tex:25** — Prefer “The G071 enable output PA10 marks the interval in which switching bursts are allowed.” This explains why its waveform is not a transistor turn-on trace. Keep the following ZVS timing limitation and equation.

6. **Chapters/09_Experimental_Methods/07_Control_Tests.tex:4–6,17** — The first sentence stacks PA1, ADC, DMA and PA10. Suggested: “For the final PWR-1.9.20 tests, the secondary-bulk voltage at input PA1 was read continuously through the ADC/DMA acquisition path. The enable output PA10 set the switching bursts.” Follow with the serial-settings explanation. In the later sentence, say “when the burst-enable signal at PA10 is low.” This names the logic signal, not an absence of stored power.

7. **Chapters/10_Results/06_ZVS.tex:4** — “PA10 groups the … carrier” gives a pin agency without explaining it. Prefer “The G071 burst-enable signal at PA10 selects groups of the nominal … switching cycles.” Keep the numerical carrier value and the waveform analysis.

8. **Chapters/10_Results/10_Firmware_Comparison.tex:4–6** — Highest-priority meaning fix: PA1 is an input and does not itself set a voltage demand. Prefer “The secondary-bulk voltage measurement at PA1 is used for regulation. The primary-current signal at PA2 classifies load, while the burst-enable output at PA10 controls average transfer. The carrier commands at PA8/PA9 keep the nominal switching frequency.” This improves both accuracy and readability without changing the controller.

9. **Chapters/11_Discussion/03_Control_Contribution.tex:4–11** — Name the resources before their labels: “The MCU’s carrier timer, TIM1, generates the … switching pattern, while the burst-enable output PA10 selects the transfer intervals.” Describe ADC samples as converted sensor readings and DMA as the automatic transfer of those readings to memory. The intro can define the terms, but this recap connects them to the discussion’s engineering conclusion.

10. **Chapters/11_Discussion/08_Lessons_Learned.tex:4** — “The PA2 correction” is not meaningful standalone. Prefer “The correction to the interpretation of the primary-current input PA2…” or “The primary-current sensing correction…”. Preserve the lesson that voltage and current must refer to the same circuit path before their product represents that path’s power.

11. **Backmatter/Appendices/C_Firmware_Documentation.tex:32–38** — Add a short reading key immediately before the pin-map table, with a cross-reference to sec:intro-controller-basics. Explain “package” = physical terminal number; PA/PB/PC = logical port names; TIM = timer block; CH = timer output channel; ADC channel = measurement multiplexer input; AF = the alternate-function selection connecting a pin to an internal peripheral. Clarify that GPIO means software-controlled digital input/output, RX is receive and TX is transmit. These labels are separate naming systems; do not imply that “channel 1” always means PA1 or physical pin 1. Keep all assignments/table values intact.

12. **Backmatter/Appendices/G_Software_Tools.tex:16–23** — A USB-to-UART adapter deserves a plain description: it converts the computer’s USB data connection into the controller’s serial transmit/receive signals. It does not implement the converter control loop and is not necessarily electrically isolated. Keep the existing primary-referenced-controller isolation requirement. At lines 54–58, identify “secondary-bulk voltage input PA1” and “primary-current input PA2” before using the labels for telemetry interpretation.

No new pin numbers, reference voltages, device limits, code behavior or test results are needed for these changes. Later mentions do not require a full repeated definition once the primer and first local use are clear. Raw code listings and schematic labels can remain exact; surrounding explanatory prose should provide the reading key.
