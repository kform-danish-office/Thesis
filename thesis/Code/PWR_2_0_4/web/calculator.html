Charge-pump sizing

THESIS · CLAMPED TRIANGULAR-CURRENT APPROXIMATION
Estimated pump inductance—
Natural LC frequency · fp—
Lp = 1 / (16 Cp fₛ²)

The thesis sizes the clamped charge pump using triangular current. Its natural LC frequency is fp = 2fₛ/π for this sizing relation; it is not the switching carrier. Cp is the equivalent pump capacitance, separate from the resonant-tank capacitor. This approximation does not verify clamp timing, current, or device ratings. Source: Chapter 4, “Pump Inductor Design” (05_Pump_Inductor.tex).

Starting values: Lp 33 µH, Cp 2 nF, fₛ 1 MHz. The selected unknown is solved from the other two values.

      Equations & assumptions

      fᵣ = 1 / (2π√(LΣCeq)); LΣ = L + Llk.

      LΣ = 1 / ((2πfᵣ)²Ceq); Ceq = 1 / ((2πfᵣ)²LΣ).

      Z₀ = √(LΣ/Ceq); X = 2πfₛLΣ − 1/(2πfₛCeq).

      For positive series R: Q = Z₀/R, BW = R/(2πLΣ), and normalized current = R / √(R² + X²).

      The tolerance interval uses the low and high corners of the entered L and C tolerances. No component correlation, temperature drift, voltage coefficient, or measured spread is assumed.

      A simple LC / series-RLC check cannot predict complete converter gain or efficiency. The separate Lm estimate uses f_open = 1 / (2π√((LΣ + Lm)Ceq)), Xm = 2πfₛLm, and Lm/LΣ. It assumes a linear magnetizing branch and an open secondary (nonconducting rectifier). It is not a loaded operating frequency or converter gain. Rectification, charge-pump switching, losses and parasitics require a fuller model. fₛ above fᵣ alone does not establish ZVS.
