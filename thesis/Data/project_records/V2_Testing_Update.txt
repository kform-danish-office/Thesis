My half bridge is achieving ZVS switching due to it no longer heating up like crazy anymore. But yep, systems for V2 are all good to go.

Interpretation used in the thesis:

- The correction is interpreted as approximately 60 ns programmed dead time,
  replacing the earlier 16 ns statement. The interpretation was stated to the
  user. No scope measurement or firmware version was supplied with the note.
- The external series resonant inductor is identified without calling it a
  secondary-side component: the available V2 drawing places L4 in the primary
  resonant path. The reported approximately 9.4 uH bench value is not silently
  equated with the drawing's nominal 10 uH part or the earlier V1.0 assembly.
- Approximately 1.66 nF is retained from the dictation. The preceding discussion
  reported 1.667 nF; these are treated as approximate reports of the tank change,
  not as independently measured capacitor values.
- Lower half-bridge heating is an observation. ZVS was the user's inference;
  it has not been established here from synchronized switching waveforms.
- The reported limited copper is a suspected contributor to bridge heating.
  No measured trace resistance, loss allocation, or completed layout comparison
  was supplied. Replacing the diodes was reported not to resolve the heating.
- The wider copper and possible relocation remain proposed changes.
