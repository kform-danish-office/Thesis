Primary electrical data: original workbook Load Data sheet; workbook not modified. Original recorded voltages/power retained, derived values recomputed.
Prior presentation waveform exports in outputs/input_voltage_current_240v_10w and outputs/input_voltage_current_95w were filtered, offset-adjusted and rescaled by earlier scripts to force target power and/or RMS voltage. They were excluded. Raw waveform copies are byte-identical to original exports and have SHA-256 digests.
Harmonic tables were manually transcribed and visually verified against original ITECH screenshots for all orders 1–40.
AC detail columns often contain ranges of Vavg measurement statistics, not waveform peak-to-peak ripple. Labels preserve this distinction. The 15 V/1 A workbook field 27.3799 mV differs from the explicitly noted Vpp/detail 50.582 mV; neither is substituted.
Thermal figures use the maximum marker, separately from cursor and reference marker, from the retained reference table; no stabilization time or calibrated component identity is assumed.
