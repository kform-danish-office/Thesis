\section{Supplied Magnetics and Integration Work}
\label{sec:mag-provenance}

A planar inductor and transformer from earlier DTU work by Bima Nugraha Sanusi and the DTU Power Electronics group were used in this converter. The components were measured by M\'ani Gautason.

The earlier design work covered core selection, winding geometry, air-gap studies, and loss calculations using MATLAB and two-dimensional FEM in COMSOL Multiphysics 6.2 \cite{comsol62}. The supplied cores and windings were integrated into the converter. Work in this thesis covered their electrical connections, mechanical mounting, and use in the converter calculations.
