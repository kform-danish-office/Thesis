\subsection{Mounting the Magnetics}
\label{sec:mag-mounting}

The cores and winding PCBs were mounted as subassemblies. Terminal positions and dimensions constrained the main-board interface and surrounding assembly/rework access.

Mechanical support must avoid loading solder joints or altering the core gap through mounting pressure and PCB bending. A repeatable assembly requires a fastening procedure and an air-gap check after mounting.
