Both inductors are close to the \SI{33}{\micro\henry} target. Transformer leakage is about \SI{16.5}{\micro\henry}, magnetizing inductance spans approximately \SI{140}{\micro\henry} to \SI{151}{\micro\henry}, and the turns ratio is near 8:1. Leakage was included in selecting the additional series inductance.

The fitted A/B combination is unknown, so calculations use the supplied range or a stated nominal value with the \cref{eq:app-turns-ratio} convention. AC-resistance loss estimates also require the original measurement frequency and fixture. Identifying and remeasuring the fitted parts would resolve these uncertainties.
