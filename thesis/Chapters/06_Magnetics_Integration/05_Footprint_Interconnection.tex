\subsection{Footprints and Winding Connections}
\label{sec:mag-footprint}

Pads, vias, terminal links, and return paths form part of the resonant loop, adding resistance and inductance. Footprint selection therefore affects electrical behavior as well as mounting.

Transformer polarity must match the secondary rectifier connections. The schematic names the winding nodes, but the available information does not map every terminal of the installed A/B variant. The terminals and winding polarity must be checked before the assembly can be reproduced; turns ratio alone is insufficient.
