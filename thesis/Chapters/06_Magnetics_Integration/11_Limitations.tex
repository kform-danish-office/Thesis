\subsection{Remaining Measurements}
\label{sec:mag-limitations}

The approximately \SI{33}{\micro\henry} pump inductance and \SI{16.5}{\micro\henry} transformer leakage provided the initial design values. Interconnect and mounting effects remain part of the installed impedance.

Further characterization requires fitted-part and terminal identification, in-circuit inductance measurement, complete-path isolation checks, and a stabilized thermal run. These measurements are needed to reproduce the assembly and reduce its losses.

Secondary-winding heating and limited power increase at higher load made transformer optimization a priority. The operating prototypes retained the supplied magnetics; replacement planar parts were not installed within the project time. The smaller V3 layout still needs to be built and tested.
