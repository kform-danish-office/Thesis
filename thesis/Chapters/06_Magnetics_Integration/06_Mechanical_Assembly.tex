\subsection{Assembly Tolerance and Rework}
\label{sec:mag-mechanical}

Terminal-link movement and longer winding connections alter resonant-loop impedance. Core alignment, spacing, PCB bending, and contact changes can also affect the installed magnetic path.

Calculations use the values in \cref{tab:mag-inherited-measurements}; inductance was not remeasured after installation. Main-board and link parasitics make the calculated resonance an initial estimate; refinement requires measurement at the installed terminals.
