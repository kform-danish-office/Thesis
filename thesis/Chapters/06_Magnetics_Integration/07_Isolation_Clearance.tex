\section{Isolation at the Transformer Interface}
\label{sec:mag-isolation}

Installed creepage and clearance can be limited by solder fillets, temporary wiring, supports, exposed core surfaces, or the main PCB. Checking winding-board spacing alone is insufficient.

Main-board spacing and the laboratory insulation check are described in \Cref{sec:hw-layout,sec:hw-overview}. The full insulation check includes transformer spacing, material group, and standardized dielectric tests.
