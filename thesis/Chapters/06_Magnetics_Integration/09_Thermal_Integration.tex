\section{Thermal Environment}
\label{sec:mag-thermal}

Magnetic temperature depends on winding, core, and terminal losses, heat transferred from the GaN devices, rectifiers, auxiliary supplies, and copper, and heat removed by airflow. The images in \cref{sec:method-thermal,sec:res-thermal} show surface-temperature patterns at the tested outputs. Internal winding temperatures and temperature over time on the assembled converter are needed to assess continuous operation.
