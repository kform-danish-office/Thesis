\section{Adapting the Converter to USB-C}
\label{sec:bg-gap}

Charge-pump input-current shaping in high-frequency rectifiers and LED drivers was demonstrated in the earlier work \cite{ammar2020chargepump,ammar2021led}. In this project, the related circuit was adapted to a power supply for USB-C devices that can be connected and disconnected during operation.

The selected semiconductors, supplied magnetics, auxiliary rails, sensing, and output interface were integrated with a controller that starts before isolated feedback is available and limits no-load transfer. Connector voltage, upstream bulk voltage, input current, and temperature were measured separately to assess the combined hardware and control response.

The assembled prototype was tested with the developed voltage-control firmware. Later circuit corrections are documented in schematic V1.1. The resulting operating requirements are defined in the next chapter.
