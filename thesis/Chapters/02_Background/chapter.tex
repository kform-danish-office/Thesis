\chapter{Background and Related Work}
\label{ch:background}

The charge-pump and isolated resonant paths share one switching stage. Their interaction determines input-current shaping, power transfer, and commutation. The reference converters use this operating principle; the USB-C adaptation adds startup, no-load, and load-transient requirements.
