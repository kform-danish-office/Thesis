\section{Model Limits and Next Steps}
\label{sec:sim-limitations}

The analysis estimates pump charge-transfer scale, nominal resonance, and parameter sensitivity. DC-link ripple, magnetizing current, burst timing, and residual tank energy help explain the bench results.

A predictive prototype model requires the installed branches, load dynamics, parasitics, and timing, calibrated against startup, load-step, no-load, and switching captures. Efficiency and temperature prediction additionally require loss and thermal calibration.
