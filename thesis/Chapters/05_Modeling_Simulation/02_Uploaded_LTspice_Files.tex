\section{Supplied LTspice Circuits}
\label{sec:sim-files}

The two supplied LTspice circuits represent the high-voltage PFC rectifier: source, bridge, pump capacitor, half bridge, series tank, rectifier, and resistive load. The prototype adds isolation, a separate pump inductor, the USB-C stage, and MCU burst control, changing both topology and parameters.

Replacing the \SI{158}{\micro\henry} element alone would omit the transformer, reflected load, and revised interconnections. The predecessor regulates a high-voltage output; in the prototype, the isolated secondary bulk supply feeds the USB-C PD output stage. Comparisons must state where voltages and powers are measured and which stages and auxiliary supplies are included.

The predecessor loss calculations use a different circuit and device set. Prototype efficiency is estimated from measured input and output power, with measurement locations and timing uncertainties discussed alongside the results.
