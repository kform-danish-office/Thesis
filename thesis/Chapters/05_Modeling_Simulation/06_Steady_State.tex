\subsection{Burst and Line-Cycle Behavior}
\label{sec:sim-steady}

The model must resolve carrier-scale phase, clamp timing, and energy per active cycle, as well as average transfer over each burst and the line-cycle energy imbalance in the DC link and secondary capacitors. Nominal series resonance is near \SI{0.989}{\mega\hertz}, but turn-on phase also depends on magnetizing current, pump current, and rectifier state.

Calibration requires matched input and secondary demand, followed by separate comparisons of voltage envelopes and switching waveforms. Both comparisons are needed to assess bulk ripple and commutation current as well as mean VBUS. Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports these quantities separately.
