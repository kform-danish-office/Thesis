\section{Charge-Transfer and Resonant Models}
\label{sec:sim-objectives}

The charge-transfer calculation estimates the input-power scale, while the resonant equivalent circuit estimates active-cycle gain and phase. The supplied LTspice circuits describe the predecessor rectifier, and the given magnetic measurements provide the inductance and transformer parameters used in Chapter~\ref{ch:analysis}. The calculations account for the prototype's changed topology and installed components.

A switched prototype model is needed to examine clamp timing and burst transitions and to predict bus ripple, turn-on current, packet energy, and the response to component or command changes. Matched turn-on-current and packet-energy measurements are still needed to check it. Efficiency and temperature prediction also require calibrated loss and thermal models.
