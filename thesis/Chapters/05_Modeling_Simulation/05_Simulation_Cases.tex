\section{What the Prototype Model Needs}
\label{sec:sim-cases}

The prototype model requires the pump inductor and clamp sequence; separate transformer leakage and magnetizing branches; turns ratio, rectifier conduction, and storage; and the controller's sampling, burst timing, and isolated-feedback startup delay.

An output resistor alone does not model the USB-C PD stage's input demand as its output voltage and load change. Attachment and removal also change connected capacitance. The assumed power, impedance, and load dynamics must therefore be specified.

Model calibration should start with steady bursts at a known load, followed by line-cycle buffering, load transients, and high-impedance operation. The prototype simulation remains unfinished; the following calculations predict trends to check in testing.
