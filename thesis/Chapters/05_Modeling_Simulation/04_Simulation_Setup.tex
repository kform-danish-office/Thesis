\subsection{Simulation Settings}
\label{sec:sim-setup}

The first schematic runs to \SI{40}{\milli\second}, with data saved from \SI{20}{\milli\second}. The second runs to \SI{100}{\milli\second}, with data saved from \SI{80}{\milli\second}. Both save the final \SI{20}{\milli\second} interval, or one \SI{50}{\hertz} line period. Their ideal gate pulses have 40\% command duty and half-period displacement. These settings belong to the supplied simulations. The dead time of the final half bridge cannot be determined from them.

Fourier and measurement directives calculate input current, RMS voltage and current, power, efficiency, and PF. The supplied simulations describe the reference circuits; the final board was not simulated.

A final switched-model comparison requires settling, an evaluation interval spanning line and burst behavior, and a time step resolving commutation. Time-step convergence and energy balance must be checked before comparison with measured waveforms.
