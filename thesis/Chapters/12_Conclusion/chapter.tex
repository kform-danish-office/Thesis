\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

A USB-C power supply was developed from the original DTU converter
through V1 testing, V2 integration, and a reduced V3 edge-card layout.
The tests demonstrated output regulation and identified magnetic and
input-path heating as priorities for further development.
