\section{Control Architecture and MCU Pins}
\label{sec:intro-controller-basics}

The microcontroller (MCU) regulates the isolated secondary bulk voltage through
burst modulation of the half bridge. The bulk supply feeds
the downstream USB-C PD stage, which regulates the USB-C output voltage
to the selected PD level.

Primary-voltage feedback is connected to MCU pin PA0, secondary-bulk
feedback to PA1, and half-bridge-current sensing to PA2. The current signal
is used for load classification and optional protection. ADC sampling runs
continuously through circular DMA without synchronization to the carrier.

Table~\ref{tab:intro-controller-signals} lists the MCU pins used on the
original STM32G071 controller and the STM32F410 V2 board. The original
controller uses PA10 for burst enable. On V2, PA10 is the UART receive pin,
and PA11 provides burst enable.
