\section{From the V2 Findings to V3}
\label{sec:intro-v3}

The power circuit and burst-control method were developed on V1. V2
integrated the controller and interface functions on the power board.
During powered testing, the resonant tank was changed after heating was
observed in the series inductor. Later secondary-output tests reached higher loads with voltage droop, while heating was observed at the input bridge and secondary region.

Secondary-winding heating and the difficulty of increasing power around
the high-load operating region motivated further transformer work. The
V3 edge-card layout was then slimmed by removing the USB-PD output section
and retaining secondary rectification. A net volume reduction of about
\SI{8}{\percent} was estimated. The output function changed with that
reduction, so an external regulated USB-PD stage would have to be included
in a complete-system comparison.

New planar magnetics were not installed within the available time. V3
remained a layout prototype for further magnetic and thermal development.
The objective remains \SI{100}{\watt}. The design changes and remaining
checks are described in \cref{sec:hw-v3-plan}.
