\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

The work started with a DTU charge-pump converter and supplied planar magnetics. The hardware and controller were then built around these parts for a USB-C power supply. The main change was the load: it could be absent at startup, draw different amounts of power, or be disconnected while the converter still held energy. That change connects the circuit design, burst-control development, and tests described in this thesis.
