The pump values place ideal charge transfer in the intended power range, while series values place nominal resonance near the carrier. Magnetizing current, burst transitions, and DC-link ripple determine departures from this estimate.

Pump capacitance and line voltage set charge transfer; link capacitance buffers mains-energy ripple; the tank and ratio set secondary excitation; both branch currents determine commutation and heating. Chapter~\ref{ch:simulation} applies these relationships to the supplied models, and Chapter~\ref{ch:results} evaluates the assembled converter.
