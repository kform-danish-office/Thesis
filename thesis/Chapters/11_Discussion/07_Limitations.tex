\section{Experimental Limitations}
\label{sec:disc-limitations}

The results cover one logged AC input, selected fixed outputs, and the
tested loads. The thermal image labeled \SI{20}{\volt},
\SI{4.7}{\ampere} lacks a matched DC output-voltage measurement and
has only a nominal \SI{94}{\watt} label. Long-duration testing at the
\SI{100}{\watt} target remains to be completed.

The principal gaps are synchronized input/output and auxiliary-power
measurements, calibration, and temperature histories. Some captures lack
numerical exports. The V1 efficiency estimates exclude disputed power rows and
captures without absolute DC output voltage. A synchronized repeat with
calibrated instruments is needed to measure input, output, and auxiliary
power over the same interval.

Quantitative dynamic and switching assessment requires matched revision
tests, commutation waveforms for ZVS, and a model of the installed
hardware. The supplied simulations omit parts of the assembly and sampled
controller. Dedicated USB-PD negotiation, insulation-coordination, fault,
and conducted/radiated-emissions tests are also required before assigning
a supply rating.
