\chapter{Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

The converter and controller were developed and tested on V1. V2 integrated the hardware
and enabled leakage-only tank tests, while V3 reduced the layout by
removing the USB-PD section. The remaining questions concern magnetic and
input-path heating, sustained output, and the volume of a complete USB-C PD supply.
