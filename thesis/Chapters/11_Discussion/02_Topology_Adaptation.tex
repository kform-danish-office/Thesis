\section{Topology Adaptation for a Detachable Output}
\label{sec:disc-adaptation}

The shared half bridge couples charge-pump PFC and resonant transfer.
Charge-pump current, DC-link energy, resonant current, and output demand
interact through this stage. Secondary-bus changes can therefore affect
both line-current shape and commutation current.

Unlike the original LED load, a detachable output can switch abruptly
between substantial demand and near-zero load. The USB-C PD stage
regulates the output voltage to the selected PD level, while the MCU
regulates the secondary bulk supply feeding it. Both stages must remain within their operating
ranges during disconnection and reconnection.

Mean output voltage was maintained at the tested loads, while ripple and
input-current quality varied with load. Burst spacing and fixed losses affect
low-demand performance, while current stress and heating become more significant
at high demand. The best PF and THD were measured at the highest loads on the
\SI{20}{\volt} output. Changes to the charge pump or burst schedule should
therefore be assessed together with their effects on the secondary bulk supply, USB-C PD stage, and heating.
