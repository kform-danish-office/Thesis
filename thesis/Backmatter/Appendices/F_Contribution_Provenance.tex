\chapter{Project Contributions and Earlier DTU Designs}
\label{app:provenance}

The project began with a charge-pump topology and planar magnetics from earlier
work. The converter hardware was built around those designs, and the MCU firmware
was developed for this thesis. The assembly was brought up and tested. The table
below lists the earlier designs and the work completed in this thesis.

The thesis contribution is the implementation, control development, and
evaluation of the integrated prototype. The original charge-pump PFC and
planar electromagnetic designs remain credited to their authors. The
operating-point measurements provide a starting point for the continuous-power,
full-range, and conformance tests set out in Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion}.
